Depth

The dark is not a background. It is depth - three near-black planes at slightly different values, and the violet gradient used as light falling across them: a glow behind a surface, an edge catching colour, a fade between planes. Never a fill. One thing is lit per view; everything else recedes.

The palette

Colour & measured contrast

Every ratio below was computed from WCAG 2.1 relative luminance before the sheet was drawn, not sampled by eye. Violet on near-black is the pair the whole site rests on, and it does not clear the bar - so it was not quietly used anyway. The frozen #8d3fe8 stays exactly where it is strong (as a surface, where white on it measures 5.24:1) and a lightened same-hue token carries it where it is weak (as text on dark). That is the one adjustment on this sheet, and it changes no brand value.

The type

Scale & specimens

Montserrat for display, DM Sans for body - both self-hosted in this file, neither substituted. The sizes are the hierarchy the site already declares (C6 §3: body 18px; headings 72 / 40 / 28 / 24 / 21 / 18) carried forward as fluid clamps, so the ladder is unchanged and only its behaviour between breakpoints is new. Display type is tracked negative; body is not. ALL CAPS stays reserved for section headings and calls to action, exactly as the brand's own voice uses it.

The signature

Gradient as light

Three deployments, one rule: the gradient never fills a shape that could have been a flat colour. It is a light source, an edge catching that light, or the transition between two planes. The discipline that keeps this from becoming spectacle is quantity - one lit element per view. This block is the only place on the sheet where all three appear at once, because demonstrating them is its job.

Applied

One moment, assembled

The single light band of the whole sheet. Under LEAD DARK a light section has to earn the switch, so there is exactly one, and it is spent on the price - the brand's most concrete claim. Every ink flips with it: the muted role leaves #9f9f9f (2.65:1 on white, a fail) for #4a4a4a at 8.86:1, and the accent ink becomes the true frozen violet at 5.24:1. The price plate inside is a nested .surface-dark - the same token names flipping back - which is also how the white wordmark would survive on a light page.
