02.1 The one pairing the whole dark canvas rests on, and what it measures

Brand violet #8D3FE8 against the near-black canvas measures 3.76:1. It fails AA. Darkening the ground does not rescue it: against pure black the same violet tops out at 4.01:1, so #8D3FE8 can never be AA-compliant text on any dark ground. That is a property of the colour, not of the canvas this concept chose.

The brand colour is frozen, so it is not adjusted. It is role-bounded instead. #8D3FE8 keeps every fill, every stroke, every glow and the whole gradient, and a derived sibling carries violet wherever violet must be read on dark: #AE76EF, identical hue (267.7deg) and identical saturation (78.6%), lightness lifted from 57.8% to 70%. It measures 6.22:1 on the canvas and 4.91:1 on the elevated card.

One consequence is visible in section 04. The frozen outline button keeps its exact #8D3FE8 border and takes an #AE76EF label. The variant count stays at two, the stroke stays the brand colour, and the label becomes legible.

Geometry and elevation

--ds-r-control 5px --ds-r-surface 11px --ds-r-pill 999px

Two radii do all the work. 5px on anything you touch, 11px on anything that holds. The pill token exists and is deliberately spent on one element only, the 6px status dot in the sheet head. Shadows are directional: an inset top hairline first, then two downward stops, no spread and no halo, because a bloom on a near-black canvas reads as fog.

06Signature motif Hex lattice to technical grid

The hexagonal texture behind the live hero, abstracted down to line work. Three registers of one idea, applied sparingly: the lattice for a hero-scale moment, the fine grid as a panel material, the hairline-and-tick system as the page's structural language. There are no wave, blob or triangle dividers in this concept. An edge is a 1px rule or a change of ground, and nothing else.

A · Hex lattice A 1px #8D3FE8 stroke at 34% on the canvas, radially masked so it fades out rather than tiling to the edge. One instance per page, hero scale only.
B · Technical grid The lattice reduced to orthogonal 1px lines at 5.5% alpha on a 32px module, --ds-grid. It is the panel material, felt rather than seen. It is behind this whole sheet.
C · Rules and ticks #E3E3E3 at 0.14 alpha for structure, a solid #8D3FE8 rule for emphasis, and a tick scale that marks every fifth unit. This is the divider system. There is no second one.
