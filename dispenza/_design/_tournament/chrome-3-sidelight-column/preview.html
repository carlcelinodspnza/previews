What the frame has to carry

Two oversized groups, one always ready action, and a footer the brand already recognises.

  • Bar at restTransparent over the hero. No fill, no border, no shadow, no glow. The hero supplies the contrast.
  • Bar on scrollGlass fill, backdrop blur, a hairline border and a resting shadow, in 220ms. It never changes height, so nothing under it reflows.
  • Services and TeamNever a dropdown. Both open the same full height panel, each as its own named column with its own count.
  • MobileThe same panel becomes the drawer. The bar keeps only the wordmark and the trigger, and the two actions move to the sticky bar.
  • FooterThe dark charcoal rounded card, inset from the page edge, carrying contact, links and legal as three stacked rows.
