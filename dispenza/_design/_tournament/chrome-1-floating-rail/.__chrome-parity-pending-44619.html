What to read in this frame.

  • The compressed rail keeps its pill and its inset, so the mobile bar is the same object as the desktop one.
  • The drawer repeats the 14px surface corner and the hairline border; it does not invent a new edge language.
  • Services and Team open as in-drawer accordions, so the eight and six item groups stay in one scroll rather than pushing a second screen.
  • Nothing in the drawer glows. The single lit moment stays on the scrolled rail.
