C1Cinematic Letterbox

A single wide 21:9 film still, brand-graded and dissolving into the canvas at both edges between two fading hairlines, with one uppercase caption riding its bottom scrim and both paragraphs sitting in enormous dark air below in a two-column measure that collapses to one at 390px.

It reads sleek because it commits to one gesture and pays for it in space: a single letterbox plate, two hairlines, one caption, one button — nothing competes, and the picture ends by dissolving rather than by being cropped, which is the difference between a film still and a stock photo in a box. It fixes D's flat sameness by ranking the copy instead of repeating it: P1 gets --ds-ink (16.42:1) and an accent tick, P2 gets --ds-ink-soft (6.83:1) and a neutral --ds-line tick, so the eye is told which paragraph is the lead. Button order was kept deliberately minimal — one solid BOOK DEMO, no secondary link to dilute it — and the action row's trailing hairline runs out to the margin so the row ends as a line rather than as a stranded pill. All body copy sits off the photograph entirely; only the caption rides it, on a measured 14.4–17.4:1 scrim.

Trade-off — The 21:9 plate is a big commitment of vertical real estate for a section carrying no headline, and the layered grade (color blend + screen bloom + mesh + grain + vignette on one masked plate) is six compositing layers on a single element, which is real GPU work on a low-end phone. Repairs: constraint 4 hardened only — the caption was the one absolutely positioned box with no right bound, so `right` was added at base and at ≤1023px (proved 0-pixel visual change by before/after full-page diff at 1440 and 390 with animations neutralised), and two stale contrast figures in the comments were replaced with the values actually measured on composited pixels. Constraints 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8 needed no repair: both paragraphs byte-diffed identical including the "tour" typo and the "presence, From" splice, all 29 selector parts start with .c1 with zero !important, zero `100vw` (and zero vw units) in live CSS, scrollWidth === clientWidth at 1440/1024/768/390 with a 342px single column, document.getAnimations().length === 0 under prefers-reduced-motion, and the image src/alt are intact with the asset confirmed on disk at ../assets/in-pages/seo-specialist-search-desk.webp.

C2Edgeless Dissolve

A full-bleed photograph with no edges at all — it materialises out of the page surface, glows from a violet bloom behind the search bar, and melts back into a hexagonal mesh at the bottom, where the two-voice copy rides up into the vanished tail.

Sleekness here comes from removing objects rather than adding them: no card, no border, no radius, no scrim — three intersecting gradient masks dissolve the photo on every side, and the page's own hex lattice is revealed exactly where the image has gone, so the picture reads as site material instead of a rectangle pasted on top. It fixes D's flat-hierarchy fault without inventing a headline: the first paragraph is the claim (bright --ds-ink-on-dark, a looser 19-21px scale) and the second is the elaboration (the page's own .mc-body step, soft ink), one rung apart by size and ink alone, split by a hairline that fades at both ends. The single BOOK DEMO button sits at the foot of the second, quieter column — the reader finishes the elaboration and the ask is the last thing on the page, rather than interrupting the claim. Contrast is solved by absence: the copy lifts into the dissolved tail where the mask is at most .060 alpha, so no wash is ever laid over the photograph.

Trade-off — The whole effect rests on mask-image, so a non-masking engine falls back to a plain radiused rectangle that loses the concept entirely, and the mask stops are tuned to this specific crop — swapping the photograph means re-tuning every ramp. Repairs: constraint 5 (contrast) — `--c2-lift` capped 72px→56px, because the vw-scaled lift outran the px-capped 629px media box and put the copy's top edge at .107 mask alpha from ~1600px up, above the .07 ceiling the concept claims (now .060 worst case, 1024-3840px); constraint 7 (tokens) — three painted `rgba(0,0,0,0)` fade-out stops in the two hairline gradients swapped to the `transparent` keyword, leaving rgba used only for mask alpha (0 painted rgba remain); plus CSS comments rewritten to drop unverifiable "measured 4.87:1 / 16.75:1 / 0 pixels differ" claims and state only the geometry I actually computed. Constraints 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 and 8 were verified clean and needed no repair.

C3Refracted Sidelight

A full-bleed photographic band whose top and bottom edges dissolve into the page canvas, with one frosted glass pane floated low and left carrying the whole copy block and a single violet CTA.

Sleek here is subtraction: one photograph, one pane, one button, and no decoration that isn't doing structural work. It fixes D's two failures directly — D read as a rounded card sitting on the page, so this squares the corners, breaks out by exactly the container gutter, and dissolves the top and bottom edges into --ds-bg-surface with fade-to-paper seams so the band is cut into the page rather than placed on it; and D's flat grey wash is replaced by a violet duotone grade (mix-blend-mode:color keeps the photo's own luminance, so the brand re-tunes the picture without touching the contrast maths) plus a far lighter wash raked to the site's constant 100deg light angle. The pane is offset on two axes at once — a 58/42 split and block padding weighted ~1.6:1 top-to-bottom — so the asymmetry is composed, not accidental. On the button: one CTA only, and the accent is never ink here (violet text measures 2.26:1 on this plane), so the accent gets to be light instead — the kicker's glowing tick, the note's violet-tipped hairline, the solid pill.

Trade-off — The whole section rests on a single photograph, so a weaker or busier image swap collapses most of the concept, and at 390px the pane runs ~937px tall as one unbroken column of 17px body copy with no scale step to break it — hierarchy is carried entirely by the inset hairline, which is a long scroll for a reader who only skims. Repairs: no repairs needed — all eight constraints verified clean (copy byte-identical to source, 41/41 selectors .c3-scoped, no 100vw, 0px document overflow at 1440/1024/768/390 with the overflow guard removed, panel scrim rgba(34,36,41,.72) with measured worst-case text contrast 8.24:1, reduced-motion confirmed neutralising animation and transitions, no raw colours, image tag and alt intact).

C4Precision Split

A Swiss editorial band: a numbered, hairline-measured copy column on the left, a photograph cut hard off the right edge, and one accent-tipped seam running the full height between them.

It reads sleek because every element is a line, not a box — a 1px seam, three hairline rules, two hanging index stems, two corner registration ticks — so the band gets its structure from geometry instead of from panels, borders or cards. It fixes D's text-over-photo legibility problem at the root rather than mitigating it: the copy and the photograph occupy separate grid columns (separate stacked rows below 1024px), so no glyph ever crosses the image and no scrim is needed — verified by rect-intersection at 1440/1024/768/390, all false. Button call: one solid BOOK DEMO at the foot of the measure, no second ghost variant, because a single band should not offer a choice; order call: on mobile the plate moves to the top so the photograph opens the band and the CTA still lands last, after both paragraphs.

Trade-off — The plate is fixed at 9/8 while the copy runs long, so at desktop roughly the lower half of the right column is empty field — deliberate negative space carrying only the dissolved grid and one violet bloom, but it will read as plain emptiness on any page that isn't already using void as a device. Repairs: only constraint 7 (TOKENS) — the `.c4__field` mask stencil used `rgba(0,0,0,1)` for its opaque stop, now `#000`, since rgba() is reserved for scrim/shadow alpha; both paragraphs were already byte-identical to source (typo "improve tour" and the "online presence, From website audits" splice intact, diff-verified), all 51 selectors already start with `.c4`, zero `!important`, zero `vw` units of any kind, and headless renders at 1440/1024/768/390 showed scrollWidth === clientWidth at every width (390px band exactly 390px, measure 342px, largest computed width 335.4px), no console errors, reduced-motion resolving animation-name to `none`.

C5Hex-Masked Duotone

A dark violet band where the same photograph appears twice — blurred past recognition into atmospheric light across the whole band, and resolved sharp inside a single hexagonal aperture — with the page's hex mesh drawn as a real mask over the glow beside a three-rank column of copy.

It reads as sleek because the photograph is treated as lighting rather than wallpaper: a luminance-preserving `color` blend keeps the band's dark areas at pure --ds-bg-surface, so the negative space is real and the eye lands on one bright object — the hexagon — instead of a full-bleed stock image. It fixes D's flat undifferentiated slab by giving the copy three ranks (ruled kicker, brighter lead, soft-ink qualifier) with no invented headline, and by masking the photo away from the text rather than dropping a scrim over it, which is what keeps the ink at 15.66:1 while the picture stays fully visible. The mesh is the aura seen through a hex mask, so texture fades itself out before it reaches the paragraphs. One CTA only, `.btn--solid` unmodified; on mobile the plate takes `order:-1` so the image leads, the copy follows, and the button lands last in thumb reach — and since the plate holds nothing focusable, that visual reorder costs no tab order.

Trade-off — The effect leans on stacked mix-blend-modes, a clip-path and a 26px blur over a second decode-shared copy of a 1600px image, making this the most GPU-expensive band on the page, and the duotone ramp is hand-tuned to this one photograph — swapping the asset means re-measuring the blend stack. Repairs: constraint 3 (the literal token "100vw" appeared in two comments — reworded to "viewport-width unit"; the CSS itself never used it) and constraint 4 (the two `max-width:560px` caps in the ≤1023px block hardened to `max-width:min(560px,100%)` so no declared pixel width can exceed the content box — rendered geometry provably unchanged at 342px); additionally the code comment's claimed contrast figures were corrected to the values I actually measured (5.35/4.94, 15.66/16.45, 5.72/6.56). Constraints 1, 2, 5, 6, 7 and 8 needed no repair and were each verified by render, not by inspection.
