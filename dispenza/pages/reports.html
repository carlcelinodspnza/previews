REPORTS

01

Performance Marketing Reports

Our performance marketing reports focus on the key metrics that reflect the effectiveness of your marketing campaigns. We provide detailed data on various performance indicators such as website actions, including actions taken on your website such as form submissions, clicks, and purchases. We also track website visits, direction requests, and appearances on the Google top 3 map pack. These reports help you understand the impact of our strategies on driving engagement and attracting customers to your dispensary.

02

Search Performance Reports

We provide reports that measure the visibility and reach of your dispensary in search engine results. These reports include search impressions, which indicate how many times your dispensary appeared in search results. We also provide top 3 ranking reports, which show the keywords and phrases for which your dispensary ranks in the top 3 positions on search engines. These reports give you valuable insights into your dispensary's organic search performance and visibility..

03

Backlink Analysis Reports

Backlinks play a crucial role in improving your dispensary's online presence and search engine rankings. Our reports include an analysis of the backlinks pointing to your website. We track the number of backlinks, their quality, and their impact on your domain authority. These reports help you understand the effectiveness of our link building strategies and the overall authority of your website.

04

Website Traffic Reports

Our website traffic reports provide valuable insights into the number of visitors your website receives, the sources of traffic, and the behavior of your visitors. We track metrics such as total website visits, unique visitors, page views, and average session duration. These reports help you understand the effectiveness of your website in attracting and engaging visitors, allowing you to optimize your website's performance.
